EVIDENCE STANDARD

Check the claim,
not just the confidence.

  • Ask for a source that can be found and checked.
  • Prefer current authoritative guidance and primary sources for consequential claims.
  • Confirm that the source supports the specific statement.
  • Use your curriculum, approved local guidance and supervisors as the governing context.
  • If a source cannot be verified, treat the answer as uncertain.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

What OrthoSG
cannot establish.

  • AI output may be incomplete, outdated, biased or incorrect.
  • Practice performance does not demonstrate clinical competence.
  • Text-described examination cannot assess hands-on technique.
  • Content does not replace local protocols, supervision or clinical judgement.
  • OrthoSG is not an official medical-school, hospital or examination resource.
