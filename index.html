A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear expectations.
Better learning.

Is this an official medical-school resource?

No. OrthoSG is an independent educational resource for formative revision. It is not affiliated with a medical school or healthcare institution. Follow your own curriculum, local guidance and supervisors.

Can I use it for real patient care or an examination?

Use fictional or appropriately de-identified educational scenarios only. Do not enter patient identifiers, confidential records or restricted examination material. OrthoSG does not provide patient-specific clinical decisions, certify competence or replace supervision. Follow your assessment rules.

Will it remember my progress?

Do not rely on continuity across chats. Ask for a revision summary, check it, and manually paste it into the next conversation. Keep personal and patient identifiers out of that summary.

What if an answer seems wrong?

Ask the GPT to explain its uncertainty and provide a verifiable source. Check that the source exists and supports the claim. Use the feedback link below to report a problem without including sensitive information.
