Before you start

The diagnostic is deliberately short. Twelve questions are enough to point revision in the right direction, and are not a measure of your knowledge or your competence.

Bring the plan back next session by pasting your Revision Passport and saying “continue my revision”.

Formative education — not certification of clinical competence. Do not enter identifiable patient information.
