Before you start

Station criteria are formative and generated for practice. They are not an official marking scheme and have not been issued by any examining body.

Stations only work if you actually attempt them, aloud or in writing, before saying “finished”. Reading the feedback without the attempt is the fastest way to learn nothing.

Formative education — not certification of clinical competence. Do not enter identifiable patient information.
